# Supplementary material

Public repository (code, trained weights, metrics, figures):

https://github.com/drsaikrishnathota1/pcr-cfar-uav-sar

Reproduce metrics and figures without retraining:

`python scripts/run_all.py`

Retrain from scratch:

`python scripts/run_all.py --train`
