**Pfa-calibrated residual neural CFAR for small-target detection in cluttered UAV SAR signals**

The manuscript is an original Research Paper on onboard signal processing: a Pfa-calibrated residual neural CFAR (PCR-CFAR) for few-pixel targets in cluttered UAV synthetic aperture radar. Seven figures and five tables report an operating-point study, an SCR sweep, regional false-alarm accounting, looks-matched CA/GO/OS controls, a public TerraSAR-X clutter transfer, clutter-mismatch tests and a 5122-parameter residual head. On 40 held-out two-look K-speckle coastline scenes at design \(P_{fa}=10^{-3}\), PCR-CFAR attains \(P_d=1.00\) with empirical \(P_{fa}=6.62\times 10^{-4}\).

The work sits in the journal’s **signal processing** and **artificial intelligence** scope. It is **not** intended for the special issues “VSI: PPS-LLM-UAV” or “VSI: Multimodal Data Security.”

The primary quantitative protocol is a fully specified two-look K-speckle simulator, plus a public TerraSAR-X real-clutter transfer with injected peaks. Code, weights, metrics and figures: https://github.com/drsaikrishnathota1/pcr-cfar-uav-sar

The work has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. The author declares no competing interests.
